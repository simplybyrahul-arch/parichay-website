LEGAL DATA PROTECTION & COMMERCIAL POLICIES

Privacy Policy & Client Terms

Last updated: October 2026 · Parichay Production

1. Overview & Information Collection

Parichay Production ("we," "our," or "the Studio") respects your privacy. When you interact with our website or submit an inquiry through our project brief form, we collect information provided directly by you, including your name, brand/company name, email address, phone number, and project budget parameters.

2. Use of Information

Information collected is strictly utilized to:

  • Review project briefs and formulate accurate production proposals.
  • Schedule creative discovery calls, location scout meetings, or production shoots.
  • Communicate project milestones and milestone deliverable links.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or brand information to third-party data brokers under any circumstances.

3. Commercial Intellectual Property & Footage Rights

Unless otherwise explicitly stated in an active production contract:

  • Master Deliverables: Clients receive full commercial broadcast and digital distribution rights for final exported and approved assets upon project completion and settlement.
  • Portfolio Showcase: Parichay Production retains the non-exclusive artistic right to display finalized commercial films and wedding documentaries within our portfolio, showreels, and film festival submissions.
  • RAW Footage: Archival RAW camera files and editing project files (Davinci Resolve / Premiere Pro project files) remain the property of Parichay Production unless buyout licenses are agreed upon in writing.
