06 METHODOLOGY & WORKFLOW

Our Creative Process,
Scene by Scene.

A disciplined 8-stage methodology that bridges business objectives, cinematic craft, and digital distribution.

SCENE 01 PRE-PROD

Discovery

Deep-dive into the client's business core, target audience psychology, brand challenges, and measurable commercial goals.

Deliverable: Creative Brief & Core Narrative Anchor
SCENE 02 RESEARCH

Research

Granular market analysis, competitor benchmark study, aesthetic trends review, and local audience emotional triggers.

Deliverable: Competitor Matrix & Audience Persona
SCENE 03 STRATEGY

Strategy

Developing a bespoke creative direction that aligns cinematic storytelling strictly with actionable business objectives and ROI.

Deliverable: Creative Direction Blueprint & Channel Strategy
SCENE 04 SCRIPTING

Story Development

Concept creation, screenplay writing, dialogue development, storyboard planning, moodboards, and production design setup.

Deliverable: Final Screenplay, Storyboards & Shot List
SCENE 05 ON SET

Production

Professional cinematography, lighting design, casting direction, sound capture, on-set supervision, and meticulous execution.

Deliverable: Principal Photography & RAW 4K/6K Rushes
SCENE 06 STUDIO

Post Production

Precision offline and online editing, Foley & sound design, original background score, cinematic colour grading, and motion graphics.

Deliverable: Master Cuts in 16:9, 9:16 & 4:5 Formats
SCENE 07 ROLLOUT

Marketing & Distribution

Publishing schedule execution, targeted paid advertising campaigns, influencer amplification, and social channel management.

Deliverable: Live Multi-Channel Campaign & Ad Spend Setup
SCENE 08 IMPACT

Growth & Analysis

Continuous measurement of reach, audience retention, lead conversions, and data-backed iterative strategy refinement.

Deliverable: Performance Report & Next-Quarter Growth Plan
